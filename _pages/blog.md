A little space for life beyond research.

I love elephants and pandas, spending time in nature, exploring cities on foot, sitting in the sun, and writing fiction. I also enjoy learning about things far outside my formal training and often find myself wandering into interdisciplinary rabbit holes — from psychology and neuroscience to design, technology, and the natural world.

I am especially curious about how NLP and AI might someday contribute to wildlife conservation and help us better understand and protect animals.

- 🚶 City walks and exploring new places
- ☀️ Sitting in the sun
- ✍️ Writing fiction
