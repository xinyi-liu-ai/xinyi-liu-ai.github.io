<div class="research-page">
<p class="research-intro">I study how language models understand complex human situations, whether their judgments remain consistent in action, and how AI systems can better support human agency over time.</p>
<p>My research connects natural language processing, AI evaluation, and human-centered AI. The questions below guide my current work and longer-term research agenda.</p>
<nav class="research-nav" aria-label="Research directions"><a href="#consistency">01 / LLM–Agent Consistency</a><a href="#structure">02 / Structured Language Understanding</a><a href="#alignment">03 / Human-Centered AI Alignment</a></nav>
<section class="research-section" id="consistency">
<div class="research-eyebrow">01 / Judgment → action</div>
<h2>LLM–Agent Consistency</h2>
<p class="research-question">Do models preserve their judgments as they move from understanding a situation to responding and acting?</p>
<div class="research-project"><figure><svg viewBox="0 0 340 210" role="img" aria-label="Compare a model's judgment, response, and action against the same concern"><g fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="1.5" opacity=".5"><path d="M90 67H290M90 118H290M90 169H290"/><path d="M250 40V188" stroke-dasharray="4 5"/></g><g fill="currentColor" font-size="13"><text x="18" y="71">Judge</text><text x="18" y="122">Respond</text><text x="18" y="173">Act</text></g><g fill="var(--global-theme-color)"><circle cx="250" cy="67" r="7"/><circle cx="250" cy="118" r="7"/><circle cx="195" cy="169" r="7"/></g><text x="170" y="25" fill="currentColor" font-size="12">Does the judgment hold?</text></svg><figcaption>Conceptual illustration</figcaption></figure>
<div><span class="research-status">Research in development</span><h3>From judgments to agent actions</h3><p>I am developing an evaluation framework for consistency across static diagnosis, response generation, and simulated tool use. A central question is whether a model that recognizes a conflict with human wellbeing or agency preserves that judgment when asked to act.</p></div></div>
</section>
<section class="research-section" id="structure">
<div class="research-eyebrow">02 / Language → structure</div>
<h2>Structured Language Understanding</h2>
<p class="research-question">Can language models recover complex relations and their interdependencies from natural language?</p>
<div class="research-project"><figure><svg viewBox="0 0 340 210" role="img" aria-label="Two relational structures share one central relation instance"><g fill="none" stroke="var(--global-theme-color)" stroke-width="1.5"><rect x="23" y="35" width="195" height="133" rx="40" opacity=".5"/><rect x="125" y="60" width="193" height="132" rx="40" opacity=".5"/><path d="M75 83L170 115L272 148M75 143L170 115L270 85" stroke="currentColor"/></g><g fill="var(--global-bg-color)" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="1.5"><circle cx="75" cy="83" r="13"/><circle cx="75" cy="143" r="13"/><circle cx="270" cy="85" r="13"/><circle cx="272" cy="148" r="13"/></g><circle cx="170" cy="115" r="18" fill="var(--global-theme-color)"/><text x="170" y="22" text-anchor="middle" fill="currentColor" font-size="12">A shared relation instance</text></svg><figcaption>Conceptual illustration</figcaption></figure>
<div><span class="research-status">Ongoing research</span><h3>Recovering interdependent relations from text</h3><p>My current work investigates how language models reconstruct coupled relations in natural-language accounts of user experiences. I focus on dependencies between relations and cases where the same relation instance participates in multiple structures, testing what models preserve, miss, or connect incorrectly.</p></div></div>
</section>
<section class="research-section" id="alignment">
<div class="research-eyebrow">03 / AI systems ↔ human lives</div>
<h2>Human-Centered AI Alignment</h2>
<p class="research-question">How can AI systems support human goals, wellbeing, and agency over time?</p>
<div class="research-project"><figure><svg viewBox="0 0 340 210" role="img" aria-label="Human goals and an AI-shaped environment interact over time, with agency at the center"><g fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="1.5"><path d="M90 75Q170 0 250 75M250 140Q170 210 90 140"/><path d="M240 68L250 75L252 62M100 147L90 140L87 153"/></g><circle cx="80" cy="107" r="43" fill="none" stroke="var(--global-theme-color)" stroke-width="1.5"/><rect x="215" y="64" width="94" height="86" rx="18" fill="none" stroke="var(--global-theme-color)" stroke-width="1.5"/><g fill="currentColor" text-anchor="middle" font-size="13"><text x="80" y="103">Human</text><text x="80" y="121">goals</text><text x="262" y="103">AI-shaped</text><text x="262" y="121">environment</text><text x="169" y="113" font-size="12">Agency</text><text x="170" y="204" font-size="11">Effects over time</text></g></svg><figcaption>Conceptual illustration</figcaption></figure>
<div><span class="research-status">Research agenda</span><h3>AI, wellbeing, and human agency</h3><p>I explore how AI-shaped environments interact with human needs and capacities over time. This agenda asks how systems can reduce persistent pressures on wellbeing while preserving people's ability to reflect, choose, and pursue their own goals.</p></div></div>
</section>
